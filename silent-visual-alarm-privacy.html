Privacy Policy for Silent Visual Alarm

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Silent Visual Alarm is developed by ertbaran. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information You Provide

The app lets you create alarms and customize visual, vibration, color, image, and video settings. These settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not require an account and does not send your alarm labels, schedules, media files, or customization settings to ertbaran servers.

Advertising

Silent Visual Alarm uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share data such as IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before ads are requested. You can reset or delete your Android advertising ID from your device settings.

Permissions

The app may request permissions needed for alarm behavior, such as overlay display, exact alarms, vibration, full-screen intent, wake lock, notification-related behavior, and battery optimization settings. These permissions are used to make visual alarms work reliably.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob and related Google services. Their handling of data is described in Google's policies and developer documentation.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

Data Sharing

ertbaran does not sell your personal information. Data processed by Google AdMob may be collected or shared by Google as described above for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Children

Silent Visual Alarm is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
