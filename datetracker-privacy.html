Date Tracker APP Privacy Policy

Package: com.ertbaran.datetracker
Last updated: August 15, 2026

Date Tracker APP is published by Ertuğrul Baran. The app has no developer-operated account system or cloud backend. Event information is kept locally on the user's device unless the user deliberately shares it with another app.

EnglishTürkçe

1. Information handled by the app

You may enter event names, dates, appearance choices and an optional image. This information is stored locally on your device so the app and its widgets can display your events. Local notifications are scheduled on your device when you enable them.

The developer does not receive this event information through a developer-operated server. If you use Android's share function, the content you choose is sent only to the app or person you select. That transfer is initiated and controlled by you and is then governed by the recipient's privacy practices.

2. Advertising, consent and Google services

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show app-open advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage privacy choices where required. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

Google's advertising SDK may automatically collect or share information such as IP address, general location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers. Google states that this data may be used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Data transmitted by the SDK is encrypted in transit.

The app also uses Google Play services for in-app update and review flows. Information processed by Google is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Google provides further details in its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Your choices

Where Google requires it, a consent or privacy-choice message is shown. You can revisit available advertising choices through the app's “Privacy choices” option. You can also manage or reset your device's advertising identifier and ad personalisation controls in Android or Google settings. Refusing personalised advertising does not necessarily remove all advertising.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to request ads and communicate with Google services.
  • Notifications: used only when you allow event reminders.
  • Selected image access: Android's system picker lets you choose an optional event image without granting broad photo-library access.

5. Retention and deletion

Event information remains on your device until you delete the event, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Shared copies are controlled by the destination you selected. Advertising data retention is controlled by Google and its authorised advertising partners under their policies. You can delete local app data from Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Children

Date Tracker APP is intended for adults and its Google Play target audience is 18 and over. It is not directed to children under 13.

7. Security and international processing

The app uses Android platform protections and encrypted network connections provided by the integrated Google SDKs. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free. Google and authorised partners may process advertising information in countries other than your own, subject to their legal safeguards.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app, its SDKs or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available at this URL.

Uygulama, açılış reklamları göstermek için Google AdMob'u (Google Mobile Ads SDK), gerekli bölgelerde gizlilik tercihlerini almak ve yönetmek için Google User Messaging Platform'u kullanır. Bölgenize ve tercihinize göre kişiselleştirilmiş veya kişiselleştirilmemiş reklam gösterilebilir.

Google'ın gerekli gördüğü durumlarda izin veya gizlilik tercihleri mesajı gösterilir. Uygulamadaki “Gizlilik tercihleri” seçeneğinden mevcut reklam tercihlerinizi yeniden açabilirsiniz.
